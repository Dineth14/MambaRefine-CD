\begin{frame}{Ablation Deltas: What Actually Moved the Score}
    \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
        \begin{column}{0.53\textwidth}
            \centering
            \scriptsize
            \begin{tabular}{lrr}
                \toprule
                \textbf{Change} & \(\Delta\)\textbf{F1} & \(\Delta\)\textbf{IoU} \\
                \midrule
                A0 \(\rightarrow\) A1: MambaVision backbone & +16.58 & +25.16 \\
                A1 \(\rightarrow\) A2: unsigned D-RBI & +0.12 & +0.22 \\
                A2 \(\rightarrow\) A3: signed difference & +0.95 & +1.69 \\
                A3 \(\rightarrow\) A4: ARF-FPN & +0.08 & +0.13 \\
                A4 \(\rightarrow\) A5: BRR only & -0.77 & -1.38 \\
                \bottomrule
            \end{tabular}
        \end{column}
        \begin{column}{0.44\textwidth}
            \small
            \begin{itemize}
                \item The backbone is the dominant region-accuracy gain.
                \item D-RBI gives a small unsigned gain; signed temporal evidence gives the larger D-RBI gain.
                \item ARF-FPN gives a small region-score gain but improves boundary metrics later.
                \item Boundary residual refinement is harmful when it is added without the final supervision schedule.
            \end{itemize}
        \end{column}
    \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Backbone Scaling and Efficiency}
    \centering
    \scriptsize
    \begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Backbone} & \textbf{Params (M)} & \textbf{GFLOPs} & \textbf{F1} & \textbf{IoU} & \textbf{OA} \\
        \midrule
        MambaVision-T & 46.73 & 50.05 & 94.58 & 89.72 & 96.24 \\
        \textbf{MambaVision-S} & \textbf{65.40} & \textbf{64.08} & \textbf{95.67} & \textbf{91.71} & \textbf{96.98} \\
        MambaVision-B & 113.37 & 109.15 & 95.77 & 91.89 & 97.07 \\
        MambaVision-L & 244.76 & 222.95 & -- & -- & -- \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}

    \vspace{0.8em}
    \small
    Small \(\rightarrow\) Base gives only \(+0.10\) F1 for \(1.73\times\) parameters.
    MambaVision-S is therefore the best accuracy/complexity point in the completed runs.

    \vspace{0.3em}
    \scriptsize
    GFLOPs are an fvcore relative profile; selective scan and attention operators were reported as unsupported, so the profile is best used for relative scaling, not exact hardware throughput.
\end{frame}
