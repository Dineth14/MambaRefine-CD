\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Overview}
\ours{} predicts a binary change map from a bi-temporal image pair $(I_1, I_2)$ through five components: a shared MambaVision encoder, a four-stream temporal feature constructor, \drbi, an adaptive receptive-field FPN decoder with CRAM-lite modulation, and a bounded boundary residual refinement head. Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline} shows the overall pipeline.

\subsection{Shared MambaVision Encoding}
A shared encoder $E(\cdot)$ processes both input images in the same feature space:
\begin{equation}
    \{F_1^s\}_{s=1}^{S} = E(I_1), \qquad \{F_2^s\}_{s=1}^{S} = E(I_2).
\end{equation}
Weight sharing ensures that the temporal difference computed downstream reflects genuine change rather than encoder domain mismatch. We use MambaVision-S as the default backbone (channels $[96, 192, 384, 768]$, $S=4$ stages), initialized from pretrained weights and kept trainable. The backbone fuses Mamba layers for long-range modeling and Transformer layers for local attention~\cite{hatamizadeh2025mambavision}.

\subsection{Four-Stream Temporal Feature Construction}
At each encoder stage $s$, we apply independent GroupNorm to $F_1^s$ and $F_2^s$, then concatenate four streams:
\begin{equation}
    T^s = \bigl[F_{1,n}^s,\; F_{2,n}^s,\; |F_{2,n}^s - F_{1,n}^s|,\; F_{2,n}^s - F_{1,n}^s\bigr],
\end{equation}
where $F_{i,n}^s$ denotes the normalized feature. Absolute difference provides a symmetric change-magnitude cue; signed difference preserves the temporal direction of change, which conveys whether a structure appeared or disappeared. This four-stream concatenation $T^s$ is projected to a fixed channel width through a lightweight 1$\times$1 convolution followed by a depthwise 3$\times$3 and pointwise 1$\times$1 block, yielding the temporal descriptor $D^s$.

\subsection{Differential Region--Boundary Interaction (\drbi)}
\label{sec:drbi}
\drbi{} applies two separate gating mechanisms to $D^s$ to expose region-level and boundary-level evidence before decoding. Fig.~\ref{fig:drbi} illustrates the module.

\textbf{Region gate.}  
A spatial attention path processes $D^s$ through a depthwise 3$\times$3 convolution and a pointwise 1$\times$1 convolution to produce unnormalized gate logits, which are clamped to $[-8, 8]$ and passed through sigmoid, then scaled to bound the response:
\begin{equation}
    G_r^s = 0.6\cdot\sigma\!\bigl(\operatorname{clamp}(g_r(D^s),{-8,8})\bigr) + 0.2,
    \qquad G_r^s \in [0.2, 0.8].
\end{equation}
The lower bound prevents complete feature suppression; the upper bound prevents unrestricted amplification.

\textbf{Boundary gate.}  
A Sobel-conditioned gate modulates its response using the magnitude of image-level edges computed on $D^s$. Sobel magnitude is clamped to $[0, 10]$ and normalized, providing a spatial prior on likely boundary locations:
\begin{equation}
    G_b^s = 0.4\cdot\sigma\!\bigl(S^s \cdot g_b(D^s)\bigr),
    \qquad G_b^s \in [0.0, 0.4],
\end{equation}
where $S^s$ denotes the normalized Sobel magnitude map. The tighter bound keeps the boundary gate from dominating when edge evidence is unreliable.

\textbf{Outputs.} The two gated responses and the original descriptor are returned:
\begin{equation}
    R^s = G_r^s \odot D^s, \qquad B^s = G_b^s \odot D^s, \qquad D^s\;\text{(unchanged)}.
\end{equation}
These three streams give the decoder access to region evidence, boundary evidence, and unmodulated temporal features.

\subsection{CRAM-Lite Modulation and Adaptive Receptive-Field Decoding}
\label{sec:decoder}
At selected encoder stages ($s \in \{0,1,2\}$), a lightweight CRAM-lite block reweights the region features spatially. Following the motivation of change-region-aware attention in Mamba-CD~\cite{mambacd2026}, each CRAM-lite block estimates a per-location attention map $A \in [0,1]$ via depthwise and pointwise convolutions and applies a residual modulation:
\begin{equation}
    F_{\mathrm{out}} = F_{\mathrm{region}} \cdot (1 + \alpha \cdot A), \qquad \alpha \in \mathbb{R}^+\;\text{(learnable, init 0.5)}.
\end{equation}

The modulated multi-scale features are decoded through a top-down FPN with adaptive receptive-field blocks. Each block runs four parallel dilated depthwise convolutions with rates $\{1,2,4,8\}$, weights each branch via per-image softmax attention (estimated through global average pooling and a shared linear layer), and sums the weighted outputs. This gives the decoder access to both compact object-level changes and larger spatial structures without altering the input resolution.

\subsection{Coarse Head and Bounded Boundary Residual Refinement}
\label{sec:refinement}
The decoder first produces a coarse logit map $P_c$ through a 3$\times$3 convolution followed by a 1$\times$1 output projection, bilinearly upsampled to the input resolution. A boundary refinement head then computes a bounded correction:
\begin{equation}
    P_f = P_c + 0.1 \cdot \tanh(\Delta),
\end{equation}
where $\Delta$ is estimated from the concatenation of the coarsest boundary feature $B^0$, the coarse logit $P_c$, and Sobel edges of $\sigma(P_c)$. The $\tanh$ bounds the correction magnitude; the scalar $0.1$ limits refinement to uncertain edge regions without freely overwriting the coarse global prediction. The final linear layer of the boundary head is zero-initialized to ensure the model begins training from the coarse prediction alone.

\subsection{Training Objective}
\label{sec:loss}
The full training loss is:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BCE}} + \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Dice}}
                + 0.4\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{coarse}}
                + 0.1\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{boundary}},
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BCE}}$ and $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Dice}}$ supervise the final output $P_f$; $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{coarse}}$ applies the same binary supervision to $P_c$; and $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{boundary}}$ is a Sobel-edge $\ell_1$ loss between predicted and ground-truth boundaries. Ablation variants A0--A5 are trained with only $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BCE}} + \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Dice}}$ on the final output (coarse and boundary loss weights set to zero).
