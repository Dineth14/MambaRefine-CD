\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Deep Learning for Change Detection}
The evolution of remote sensing change detection (CD) has been largely driven by advancements in deep learning architectures. Early deep learning approaches predominantly relied on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to extract bi-temporal features. While CNN based architectures do excel at capturing local textures and spatial hierarchies, due to their limited receptive fields, their ability to model the long range dependencies necessary for identifying large scale spatial transformations is restricted. To address this limitation, subsequent research heavily adopted Vision Transformers (ViTs), which leveraged self-attention mechanisms to establish global context. However, due to the quadratic computational complexity $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ of self-attention, it poses a severe bottleneck for remote sensing tasks, where high resolution, bi-temporal image processing requires massive memory footprints and computational overhead, thereby limiting their practical deployment on edge devices.

\subsection{State Space Models in Remote Sensing}
Recently, State Space Models (SSMs), particularly the Mamba architecture, have emerged as a highly efficient alternative to ViTs. By establishing linear computational complexity $\mathcal{O}(N)$ while maintaining a global receptive field, Mamba models are exceptionally well suited for high resolution remote sensing. Foundational works such as ChangeMamba \cite{chen2024changemamba} pioneered the integration of the Visual Mamba (VMamba) architecture into bi-temporal processing, thereby demonstrating competitive performance with significantly reduced computational costs.

Building upon this, concurrent works have attempted to refine Mamba's feature extraction capabilities. For instance, HAM-CD \cite{li2026hamcd} introduced a Hybrid Attention Mamba decoder, utilizing a Transposed Attention Block to compensate for Mamba's inherent weaknesses in capturing fine grained local dependencies. Similarly, Mamba-CD \cite{mambacd2026} proposed a recursive context refinement mechanism to enhance semantic representations. While these approaches successfully improve overall region detection, they fundamentally treat change detection as a uniform semantic segmentation task. By failing to differentiate between the semantic core of a change region and its highly variable morphological edges, these models often suffer from boundary degradation and fuzzy localized predictions in complex topographies.

\subsection{Boundary-Aware \& Temporal Feature Fusion}
Precise boundary delineation remains a critical challenge in change detection due to temporal misalignments, sensor noise, and seasonal variations. While some recent frameworks, such as BSCNet \cite{zhou2026bscnet}, have emphasized boundary-aware semantic context to sharpen edge predictions, they rely on computationally heavy Transformer backbones that undermine the efficiency required for large scale analysis.

Furthermore, a critical limitation in existing literature lies in the methodology of temporal feature fusion. The vast majority of current state-of-the-art models, including recent Mamba adaptations, fuse bi-temporal features $(F_1, F_2)$ via channel concatenation or absolute differencing $|F_2 - F_1|$. Mathematically, absolute differencing "folds" the feature space, entirely obscuring the temporal direction of the change (e.g., distinguishing the construction of a new building from the demolition of an existing one). This loss of directional context deprives the decoder of crucial morphological cues.

To bridge these gaps, our proposed MambaRefine-CD explicitly preserves temporal direction by utilizing a signed temporal difference $D_s = F_2 - F_1$. Furthermore, we move beyond uniform feature decoding by introducing a Differential Region Boundary Interaction (D-RBI) module. Unlike hybrid attention mechanisms that globally process spatial features, D-RBI explicitly decouples the region response from the boundary response. Combined with a lightweight boundary residual refinement module, MambaRefine-CD achieves high fidelity edge delineation without the computational burden of heavy attention mechanisms.
