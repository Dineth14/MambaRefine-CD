\caption{\drbi{} module. The four-stream temporal descriptor $D^s$ is passed to two independent gating branches. The region gate applies a depthwise--pointwise path with sigmoid scaling bounded to $[0.2, 0.8]$. The boundary gate uses the same structure but is modulated by a normalized Sobel-magnitude spatial prior and bounded to $[0.0, 0.4]$. Both gated outputs and the original descriptor are forwarded to the decoder.}
