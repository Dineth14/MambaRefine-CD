\section{Conclusion}

This paper presented \ours, a MambaVision-based architecture for binary remote-sensing change detection. The method separates temporal evidence into low-frequency region cues and high-frequency boundary cues through \drbi, then combines signed temporal difference, adaptive receptive-field decoding, CRAM-lite attention, and boundary residual refinement. Verified results are reported for DSIFN-CD, WHU-CD, and LEVIR-CD validation only. SECOND is left as a future semantic change detection extension until its pipeline is reliable. Future work will complete verified ablations and add additional benchmarks under the same metric protocol.
